Privacy

Privacy and analytics.

This personal website uses limited analytics to understand how visitors find the site and which pages they use. It does not offer user accounts, accept payments or collect information through web forms.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Information collected automatically

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views, referring sources, approximate geographic location, browser and device type, engagement, scrolling and outbound-link activity. I receive reports produced from this data, not visitors' raw IP addresses.

Google Analytics may place first-party cookies, including identifiers such as _ga and related cookies, in a visitor's browser. These identifiers help distinguish visits and calculate aggregate traffic.

Google Signals

Google Signals is enabled only for visitors located in the United States and Nepal. For eligible visitors who are signed into Google and have consented to Ads Personalization, Google may associate site activity with information from their Google accounts and provide aggregated demographic and interest reports.

I do not receive visitors' names, email addresses, Google account identifiers, individual search histories or individual advertising profiles through these reports. Google may withhold demographic results when the audience is too small.

This site does not use Google Ads, remarketing audiences or analytics data for targeted advertising. I do not sell visitor data.

External services

The site loads Google Analytics and Google Fonts. Those services process technical information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. Links to LinkedIn, Substack, universities, publishers and other external sites are governed by the privacy practices of those sites after you leave aashisluitel.com.

Email

If you contact me by email, I receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. I use that information to read and respond to the correspondence.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking analytics cookies does not prevent you from reading this site.
