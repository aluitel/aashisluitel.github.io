Practice

Microsoft Security Copilot

Product and program leadership · Responsible AI, privacy, compliance, sovereign cloud

Security Copilot was the first generative AI security product to reach market, which meant no precedent existed for how trust, safety, and ethics should be built into one. I worked across engineering, legal, and policy to turn Responsible AI principles into practices an auditor could examine, and aligned the platform to SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 42001, and FedRAMP controls.

The durable lesson was treating regulatory expectation as a design input rather than a gate at the end. That reordering changed the architecture, not just the paperwork.
