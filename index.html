Speaking

Translating governance for the people who have to implement it

The hard part of AI governance is rarely the principle. It is the meeting where an engineer, a lawyer and a contracting officer discover they mean different things by the same word. I speak to technical, academic, policy and executive audiences about Responsible AI, CMMC, FedRAMP and what security assurance requires in operational environments.

Recent appearances include the Federal News Network Risk & Compliance Exchange in 2026, on closing the CMMC evidence gap.
